# Code of conduct

Tropos Code follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and kind, to newcomers most of all.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks, or publishing anyone's private
  information.
- Assume good faith, and take responsibility for your mistakes.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, email **contact@troposlabs.com**. Reports are read by the
maintainers only, kept confidential, and answered promptly. Security vulnerabilities are reported differently, as
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide how to respond, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on the severity and on whether it
happens again.
